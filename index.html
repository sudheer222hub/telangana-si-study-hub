📐 Maths — Topic 01: Percentages

Chapter goal: concept clear chesukoni, model identify chesi, time limit lo accurate ga solve cheyyadam. First accuracy, then speed.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Percentage ante “100 lo entha?” Example: 25% ante 100 lo 25 parts, ante one-fourth (¼). Question lo whole/base value identify cheyyadam most important.

🎯 3-step solving method
1) Given values mark cheyyi → 2) Question em adugutundo identify cheyyi → 3) Formula apply chesi answer ni estimate tho check cheyyi.

🧠 Model 1 — Direct percentage

640 lo 15% entha?

10% = 64; 5% = 32.

15% = 64 + 32 = 96

Shortcut: percentage ni easy parts ga split cheyyi.

🔎 Model 2 — Find the whole

Oka number lo 30% = 150. Number?

10% = 150 ÷ 3 = 50; 100% = 50 × 10.

Answer = 500

📈 Model 3 — Percentage increase

Price ₹400 nunchi ₹500 ki perigindi.

Increase = 500 − 400 = ₹100.

100 ÷ 400 × 100 = 25%

Denominator always original value.

📉 Model 4 — Percentage decrease

Price ₹1,000 nunchi ₹800 ki taggindi.

Decrease = ₹200.

200 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 20%

🏷️ Model 5 — Discount

₹1,200 item meeda 15% discount.

10% = 120; 5% = 60; discount = ₹180.

Final price = ₹1,020

🔁 Model 6 — Successive changes

₹100 meeda 20% increase, then 10% decrease.

100 → 120 → 108.

Net = 8% increase

20 − 10 = 10% ani direct subtract cheyyakudadhu.

🧾 Model 7 — More/less comparison

A, B kanna 25% ekkuva. B = 200.

25% of 200 = 50.

A = 250

Base B kabatti 200 meeda percentage calculate cheyyali.

↔️ Model 8 — Reverse comparison trap

A is 25% more than B. Then B is A kanna entha % less?

B = 100 anukunte A = 125. Difference = 25.

25 ÷ 125 × 100 = 20% less

Comparison base marithe percentage kuda marutundi.

🗳️ Model 9 — Pass/fail

40% marks = 160. Total marks?

1% = 160 ÷ 40 = 4.

100% = 400 marks

👥 Model 10 — Population/salary

Salary ₹20,000; 12% increase.

Increase = 10% (₹2,000) + 2% (₹400).

New salary = ₹22,400

⚠️ Common mistakes
• Increase/decrease percentage calculate chesetappudu original value ni denominator ga use cheyyi.
• “A is 20% more than B” and “B is 20% less than A” same kaavu.
• Two successive percentage changes ni simply add/subtract cheyyaku.
• Units and arithmetic final ga check cheyyi.

🧠 Maths Formula Revision Zone

Formula marchipothe tension avvaku. Ee section ni daily 5–10 minutes revise cheyyi. Formula ni chadavadam tho paatu, book close chesi gurthu nunchi cheppadam practice cheyyi.

Recall method: Formula chaduvu → example choodu → screen cover chesi formula rayi → one question solve cheyyi.

📊 Percentage

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Use when: “A is what percent of B?”

Example: 40 is what % of 200? → 40 ÷ 200 × 100 = 20%.

➗ Average

Average = Total sum ÷ Number of items

Use when: average or total is asked.

Example: 4, 6, 10 average = 20 ÷ 3 = 6⅔.

🚗 Speed, Distance, Time

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

Distance = Speed × Time; Time = Distance ÷ Speed.

Example: 120 km in 3 hours → speed = 40 km/h.

💰 Simple Interest

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100

P = principal, R = annual rate %, T = years.

Example: ₹1,000 at 5% for 2 years → SI = ₹100.

🛍️ Profit & Loss

Profit = SP − CP
Loss = CP − SP

Profit % = Profit ÷ CP × 100. Loss % = Loss ÷ CP × 100.

Remember: percentage base is usually CP.

🧱 Time & Work

1-day work = 1 ÷ days needed

If A finishes in 5 days, A's one-day work = 1/5.

Together: add their one-day work rates, when working independently at constant rates.

⚖️ Ratio

a : b means a/b

If ratio is 2:3, total parts = 5. Split total by 5, then multiply by 2 or 3.

🧮 Quick calculation

x% of N = (x/100) × N

Use easy fractions when possible: 25% = 1/4, 12.5% = 1/8, 20% = 1/5.

🔁 Formula recall drill

1. 25% of 360?
25% = 1/4. 360 ÷ 4 = 90.
2. 5 items sum is 75. Average?
Average = 75 ÷ 5 = 15.
3. 150 km in 3 hours. Speed?
Speed = distance ÷ time = 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.
4. CP ₹400, SP ₹460. Profit percentage?
Profit = ₹60. Profit % = 60 ÷ 400 × 100 = 15%.
5. A completes work in 8 days. One-day work?
1/8 of the work per day.

⚖️ Maths — Topic 02: Ratio & Proportion

First basics nunchi start. Ratio lo main skill: parts count cheyyadam, same units use cheyyadam, and required value ni identify cheyyadam.

Ratio a : b = a/b

Ratio ante rendu quantities ni compare cheyyadam. Example: 2 red pens, 3 blue pens unte red : blue = 2 : 3. Order important — blue : red = 3 : 2.

🎯 4-step method
1) Quantities same units lo unnayo check cheyyi → 2) Ratio simplify cheyyi → 3) Total parts calculate cheyyi → 4) One part value find chesi required parts tho multiply cheyyi.

📌 Formula & rules sheet

Simplify ratioa:b → divide both by HCFSmallest whole-number ratio kosam
Split a totalOne part = Total ÷ (sum of ratio parts)Total amount ratio lo divide cheyyali ante
Proportiona:b = c:d means ad = bcMissing term find cheyyadaniki
Direct proportionx₁/y₁ = x₂/y₂One quantity perigite other kuda same direction lo changes
Inverse proportionx₁y₁ = x₂y₂One quantity perigithe other taggutundi (same work, fixed conditions)

🟦 Model 1 — Simplify a ratio

24 : 36 simplify cheyyi.

HCF of 24 and 36 = 12. Divide both terms by 12.

24 : 36 = 2 : 3

Ratio terms ni different numbers tho divide cheyyakudadhu.

🟩 Model 2 — Split total in a ratio

₹600 ni 2:3 ratio lo divide cheyyi.

Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5. One part = 600 ÷ 5 = ₹120.

Shares = ₹240 and ₹360

🟨 Model 3 — Find a missing term

4 : 7 = 20 : x. Find x.

4/7 = 20/x. Cross multiply: 4x = 140.

x = 35

🟪 Model 4 — Ratio after adding

A:B = 2:3. Total = 50. Find A and B.

Total parts = 5; one part = 50 ÷ 5 = 10.

A = 20, B = 30

🟥 Model 5 — Age ratio starter

Ages ratio 3:5; total age 32 years.

Total parts = 8; one part = 32 ÷ 8 = 4.

A = 12 years, B = 20 years

🚰 Model 6 — Direct proportion

5 notebooks cost ₹100. Cost of 8 notebooks?

One notebook = 100 ÷ 5 = ₹20.

8 × ₹20 = ₹160

👷 Model 7 — Inverse proportion

4 workers finish a job in 12 days. Same rate: 8 workers need how many days?

Workers × days = constant. 4 × 12 = 8 × d.

d = 6 days

Assumes same work rate and all workers work equally.

🧪 Model 8 — Combine ratios

A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5. Make B equal: LCM(3,4)=12.

A:B = 8:12; B:C = 12:15.

A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15

✍️ Practice bank — tap each question to reveal the answer

1. Simplify 18:30.
HCF = 6. Divide both terms: 3:5.
2. Divide ₹840 in the ratio 3:4.
Total parts = 7; one part = 840 ÷ 7 = 120. Shares = ₹360 and ₹480.
3. If 5:8 = x:40, find x.
5/8 = x/40 → 8x = 200 → x = 25.
4. A:B = 7:9 and their total is 128. Find A and B.
Total parts = 16; one part = 8. A = 56, B = 72.
5. 6 pens cost ₹90. What do 10 pens cost at the same rate?
One pen = ₹15; 10 pens = ₹150.
6. 12 workers take 10 days. How many days for 15 workers at the same rate?
12 × 10 = 15 × d → d = 8 days.
7. A:B = 3:4 and B:C = 2:5. Find A:B:C.
Make B=4: second ratio multiply by 2 → B:C=4:10. Combined = 3:4:10.
8. Boys:girls = 5:4. Total students = 45. How many girls?
Total parts = 9; one part = 5. Girls = 4 × 5 = 20.
9. If a:b = 4:7 and a = 28, find b.
4 parts = 28, so 1 part = 7. b = 7 parts = 49.
10. Are 6:9 and 10:15 equivalent ratios?
6:9 = 2:3 and 10:15 = 2:3. Yes.
⚠️ Common mistakes
• Ratio order is important: boys:girls is not girls:boys.
• Convert units first (for example, metres and centimetres).
• Total parts = add ratio terms; difference questions use subtraction.
• In inverse proportion, multiply the paired quantities only when the conditions support it.

📊 Maths — Topic 03: Average

Average questions lo main idea: total sum entha? Enni values unnayi? Ee rendu clear ayithe most questions easy avuthayi.

Average = Sum of all values ÷ Number of values
Memory trick: Average × Number of values = Total sum. Ee reverse formula missing value questions lo chala useful.

📌 Formula sheet

Question typeFormula / method
Basic averageAverage = Sum ÷ Count
Find total sumSum = Average × Count
New number addedNew average = (old sum + new number) ÷ new count
Number removedNew average = (old sum − removed number) ÷ new count
Consecutive numbersFor evenly spaced consecutive values, average = (first + last) ÷ 2
Average changes by d when one value replacedChange in total = d × number of values

🟦 Model 1 — Basic average

12, 16, 20, 24 average?

Sum = 12 + 16 + 20 + 24 = 72. Count = 4.

Average = 72 ÷ 4 = 18

🟩 Model 2 — Find missing number

5 numbers average 14. Four numbers sum to 52. Find fifth.

Total sum = 14 × 5 = 70.

Missing number = 70 − 52 = 18

🟨 Model 3 — Add a new value

6 students average 70 marks. One more student scores 84. New average?

Old sum = 6 × 70 = 420. New sum = 504. Count = 7.

New average = 504 ÷ 7 = 72

🟪 Model 4 — Remove a value

8 numbers average 15. Remove number 22. New average?

Old sum = 8 × 15 = 120. Remaining sum = 98; count = 7.

New average = 98 ÷ 7 = 14

🟥 Model 5 — Replacement

Average of 10 players increases by 2 when one player is replaced. How much did total change?

Change in total = change in average × count.

2 × 10 = 20 marks increase

🔢 Model 6 — Consecutive numbers

Average of 11, 12, 13, 14, 15?

Values equally spaced, so average = (first + last) ÷ 2.

(11 + 15) ÷ 2 = 13

👨‍👩‍👧 Model 7 — Combined groups

20 students average 60; 30 students average 70. Combined average?

Total = 20×60 + 30×70 = 3,300. Students = 50.

Combined average = 3,300 ÷ 50 = 66

🎯 Model 8 — Target average

5 tests average 68. What score in test 6 is needed for average 70?

Required total = 6×70 = 420. Existing total = 5×68 = 340.

Required score = 420 − 340 = 80

✍️ Practice bank — tap to reveal answer & explanation

1. Find average of 18, 22, 26, 30.
Sum = 96; count = 4. Average = 24.
2. Average of 7 numbers is 12. Find their sum.
Sum = average × count = 12 × 7 = 84.
3. Average of 6 numbers is 15. Five numbers sum to 68. Find the sixth.
Total = 6 × 15 = 90. Missing = 90 − 68 = 22.
4. Average age of 4 people is 25. A person aged 30 joins. New average?
Old total = 100; new total = 130; count = 5. Average = 26.
5. Average of 9 values is 40. One value 58 is removed. New average?
Old total = 360; remaining total = 302; count = 8. Average = 37.75.
6. Average of 1 to 9?
Consecutive values: (1 + 9) ÷ 2 = 5.
7. 15 boys average 50 marks; 25 girls average 60. Combined average?
Total = 750 + 1,500 = 2,250; count = 40. Average = 56.25.
8. Average of 8 numbers is 24. If one number 17 is replaced by 33, new average?
Total rises by 16. Average rises by 16 ÷ 8 = 2. New average = 26.
9. Average after 4 matches is 35. What score in 5th match gives average 40?
Required total = 5 × 40 = 200. Existing = 4 × 35 = 140. Score = 60.
10. Average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 18. Find the numbers.
Middle number is average 18. Numbers are 14, 16, 18, 20, 22.
⚠️ Common mistakes
• Average is not always one of the given numbers.
• Find total first using average × count before adding/removing values.
• Combined averages must be weighted by group size; do not simply average the two averages unless group sizes are equal.
• Count changes when a value is added or removed.

CP = Cost Price (konugol chesina price); SP = Selling Price (ammindhi); MP = Marked Price (tag chesina price).

Profit = SP − CP | Loss = CP − SP
TypeFormula

DiscountMP − SP
Discount %Discount ÷ MP × 100

Model 1 — Profit

CP ₹800, SP ₹920. Profit = 920−800 = ₹120.

Profit % = 120/800 ×100 = 15%

Model 2 — Loss

CP ₹1,200, SP ₹1,020. Loss = ₹180.

Loss % = 180/1200 ×100 = 15%

Model 3 — Find SP

CP ₹600, profit 20%.

20% of 600 = ₹120.

SP = ₹720

Model 4 — Discount

MP ₹2,000; discount 15%.

Discount = ₹300.

SP = ₹1,700

Practice bank

1. CP ₹500, SP ₹575. Profit %?
Profit ₹75; 75/500×100 = 15%.
2. CP ₹900, loss 10%. SP?
900×90/100 = ₹810.
3. MP ₹1,500, SP ₹1,200. Discount %?
Discount ₹300; 300/1500×100 = 20%.
4. An item sold for ₹660 at 10% profit. CP?
660 is 110%; CP = 660×100/110 = ₹600.
5. CP ₹2,400, profit 12.5%. SP?
12.5% = 1/8; profit ₹300; SP = ₹2,700.

🏦 Maths — Topic 05: Simple & Compound Interest

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100

P = principal, R = annual rate %, T = years. SI lo every year interest original principal meeda calculate chestham.

Model 1 — Simple interest

₹5,000 at 8% for 2 years.

SI = 5000×8×2/100.

SI = ₹800; Amount = ₹5,800

Model 2 — Find rate

P ₹2,000; SI ₹300; time 3 years.

R = SI×100/(P×T).

R = 5% p.a.

Model 3 — Compound interest

₹1,000 at 10% annually for 2 years.

Year 1: 1100; Year 2: 1210.

CI = ₹210
Memory: Amount = Principal + Interest. Compound interest lo next year interest, previous amount meeda calculate avuthundi.

Practice bank

1. SI on ₹3,000 at 5% for 4 years?
3000×5×4/100 = ₹600.
2. P ₹4,000, R 6%, SI ₹720. Time?
T = 720×100/(4000×6) = 3 years.
3. CI on ₹2,000 at 10% for 2 years?
Amount = 2000×1.1×1.1 = 2420; CI = ₹420.
4. SI on ₹1,500 at 8% for 6 months?
T = 0.5 year; SI = 1500×8×0.5/100 = ₹60.
5. SI ₹450 on ₹2,500 for 3 years. Rate?
R = 450×100/(2500×3) = 6%.

🛠️ Maths — Topic 06: Time & Work

If A finishes in x days, 1-day work = 1/x

Workers same speed tho work chesthe, combined rate add cheyyali. Days ekkuva ante daily work rate takkuva.

Model 1 — Together

A takes 10 days, B takes 15 days.

One-day work = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6.

Together = 6 days

Model 2 — Workers & days

8 workers finish in 12 days. 6 workers need?

Workers×days = constant.

8×12 = 6×d → 16 days

Model 3 — Find remaining work

A can finish in 8 days. A works for 3 days.

Completed = 3/8.

Remaining = 5/8

Practice bank

1. A 12 days, B 12 days. Together?
Rate = 1/12+1/12=1/6. 6 days.
2. A 6 days, B 3 days. Together?
Rate = 1/6+1/3=1/2. 2 days.
3. 15 workers take 8 days. 10 workers take?
15×8=10×d; d = 12 days.
4. A completes in 20 days. Work done in 5 days?
5/20 = 1/4 work.
5. A 10 days, B 20 days. Together?
Rate = 1/10+1/20=3/20; time = 20/3 days = 6⅔ days.
Common trap: Together work time is less than the fastest individual time, when both work independently on the same job.
