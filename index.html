📐 Maths — Topic 01: Percentages

x% of N = (x ÷ 100) × N

Percentage ante 100 lo entha part. Quick calculations kosam common fractions gurthupettuko.

🧠 Model 1 — Direct percentage

640 lo 15% entha?

10% = 64; 5% = 32.

15% = 64 + 32 = 96

Shortcut: 15% ni 10% + 5% ga split cheyyi.

🔎 Model 2 — Find original number

Oka number lo 30% = 150. Number?

10% = 150 ÷ 3 = 50.

100% = 50 × 10 = 500

📈 Model 3 — Increase

₹400 nunchi ₹500 ki price perigindi.

Increase = ₹100.

100 ÷ 400 × 100 = 25%

📉 Model 4 — Decrease

₹1,000 nunchi ₹800 ki price taggindi.

Decrease = ₹200.

200 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 20%

🔁 Model 5 — Successive change

20% increase, then 10% decrease. Original ₹100 anuko: ₹100 → ₹120 → ₹108.

Net result = 8% increase

Trap: 20 − 10 = 10% ani direct ga subtract cheyyaku.

✍️ Short-answer practice

Answer reveal cheyyadaniki question meeda tap cheyyi.

1. 480 lo 25% entha?
480 ÷ 4 = 120.
2. 750 lo 12% entha?
10% = 75; 2% = 15; total = 90.
3. Oka number lo 25% = 75. Number?
75 × 4 = 300.
4. Salary ₹12,000 nunchi ₹15,000 ki perigindi. Increase %?
Increase ₹3,000. 3,000 ÷ 12,000 × 100 = 25%.
5. Price ₹600 nunchi ₹510 ki taggindi. Decrease %?
Decrease ₹90. 90 ÷ 600 × 100 = 15%.
6. 10% increase, then 10% decrease. Overall change?
100 → 110 → 99, so 1% decrease.
7. ₹800 item meeda 25% discount. Final price?
Discount ₹200; final = ₹600.
8. Oka number lo 40% = 200. Aa number lo 65%?
Number = 500; 65% = 325.
